What I bring

Someone who reads the invoice and the code.

Most automation in finance fails in the gap between the people who know the process and the people who build the tool. I sit in that gap: I can read a supplier invoice, a VAT summary or an audit finding, and I can write the program that checks it.

  • Systems analysis: I start from the process (procure-to-pay, pricing, margin control), write down the rules, and only then automate them.
  • Automation people can trust: tests, audit trails, backups, and a way to undo every change. A result that cannot be checked is not finished.
  • Honest numbers: when something does not work, I measure it and say so, as in my test of an AI forecasting model.
