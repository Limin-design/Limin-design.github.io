About

Business problems, solved with software that can be checked.

I study Business Management at ISCAL in Lisbon, finishing in 2027, and I run operations and systems at my family's minimarket. There I built a system that reads supplier invoices, proves them against the Portuguese fiscal QR code and proposes new prices for a person to approve.

What I care about is the part that makes automation safe: tests, audit trails, backups and a way to undo every change.
